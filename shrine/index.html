For grown-ups

Practice knight and rook moves on a 6 × 6 playfield. This short lesson does not include kings or checkmate.

  • You move once, then the golem moves once.
  • The golem captures the knight if it can along a straight line. Otherwise, it follows its patrol route.
  • Tap the golem to see its attacks. Red lines warn of danger; they do not forbid a move.
  • Undo restores both your move and the golem move. There is no penalty.
  • The last lesson hides move guides at first. Hints are always available.

Narration uses an English voice on your device. If none is available, follow the visual demo or use a device with an English voice installed. Buttons and demos also work with sound off.

Completed lessons are saved in this browser.
